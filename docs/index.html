Mocknik

Fill any web form with realistic fake data.

Mocknik fills forms in one click and lets you pin exact values with per-field rules. Built for developers and QA teams — and it runs entirely in your browser.

  • One click, or Alt+Shift+F to fill every field on the page
  • Literal values, templates, generators, mirrors, and skips
  • No accounts, no network requests, no telemetry
